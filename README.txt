This was another assignment from my operating systems course. This assignment drove me absolutely crazy, but it gave me some valuable experience with threads in python.
It is meant to simulate a series of train stations in which only one train can cross the main track at a time, with trains separated into high and low priority. 
As far as I understand, the aim of this assignment was meant to demonstrate how threads and mutexs work, which I think it did a fair job of.
